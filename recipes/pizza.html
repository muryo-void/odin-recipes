Classic Pizza Margherita

Ingredients:

  • 1 ball fresh pizza dough (about 250–300 g, at room temperature)
  • 120 ml crushed San Marzano tomatoes (or good-quality canned tomatoes)
  • 125 g fresh mozzarella, sliced and patted dry
  • 1 handful fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt
  • Semolina or flour, for dusting

Steps:

  1. Place a pizza stone or inverted baking sheet in the oven and preheat to the highest temperature possible (250°C–275°C / 480°F–525°F) for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Stir the sea salt into the crushed tomatoes—no cooking needed for authentic Margherita sauce.
  3. On a lightly floured surface, gently stretch the dough from the center outward into a 28–30 cm circle, leaving a slightly thicker rim for the crust. Transfer onto parchment paper dusted with semolina.
  4. Spread a thin, even layer of tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a 2 cm border. Arrange the mozzarella slices evenly on top.
  5. Slide the pizza onto the hot stone or baking sheet and bake for 7–10 minutes until the crust is puffed and charred in spots and the cheese is bubbling.
  6. Remove from the oven, top immediately with fresh basil leaves and a drizzle of olive oil, slice, and serve hot.
